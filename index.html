Privacy Policy

App: Olen (app.olen.focus) · Effective Date: November 24, 2026
#AccessibilityService API #Data Deletion #Contact Olen App

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Olen App handles information when you use the Olen Android application (package ID: app.olen.focus).

Olen is designed with a local-first privacy architecture. App blocking, short-form video blocking, website filtering, and daily screen time calculations run strictly on your device. We never sell your personal data and never display third-party behavioral ads.

100% ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

2. Android AccessibilityService API Disclosure

Olen uses the Android AccessibilityService API as its core focus and blocking engine. When enabled by you, this service operates in the background—even when Olen is closed or not actively open—for the following strictly limited purposes:

  • App & Short-Form Video Blocking: Detects the currently active app package name and short-form video feeds (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Stories) so Olen can close or interrupt distracting content you chose to restrict.
  • Website Filtering: Inspects the visible URL bar in supported web browsers solely to check domains against your personal blocklist.
  • Optional Commitment Lock: If you explicitly activate a Removal Delay or Guardian PIN lock, detects when Android Settings is opened to uphold your chosen commitment until the timer expires or your PIN is entered.

Strict Privacy Guarantee: All AccessibilityService inspection runs exclusively in memory on your device. Olen never records keystrokes, reads passwords or private messages, captures screenshots, or transmits any screen content or browsing history to any server or third party.

3. Other Android Permissions Used

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used on-device to measure your screen time, phone unlocks, and night usage to calculate your Olen Score and 1v1 match standings.
  • Installed Apps List (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Used locally to display your installed applications so you can select which apps to block, limit, or include in the optional Olen Zen Launcher. Your app list is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Notification Access (Optional): Used locally if you enable Notification Batching to hold non-urgent notifications during focus periods. Notification contents are never transmitted off your device.
  • Activity Recognition & Location (Optional): Used locally only if you enable optional movement/step challenges (e.g., Walk for Freedom) or location-based focus rules.
  • Device Administrator (Optional): Used only if you explicitly enable optional lock protection features.

4. Data Stored in the Cloud (Optional Features)

If you use Olen's optional cloud, social, or competitive features, minimal data is synced to our backend infrastructure (powered by Supabase):

  • Olen Account & Cloud Backup (Optional): If you sign in, we store your account identifier (email/auth ID) and your focus settings/score backup so you can restore them across devices.
  • Squads, Friend 1v1 Duels & Olen Ranked 1v1 (Optional): If you join a Squad, 1v1 Duel, or Ranked match, we sync your chosen display nickname, Olen Score / Elo rating, match screen-time minutes, and unlock count so your match standings can be calculated and shown to your opponent or squad members.
  • Live Global Percentile Rank (Anonymous): To calculate your "TOP X%" badge, Olen sends only a randomly generated, one-way SHA-256 anonymous device hash and your numeric Olen Score. This hash cannot be traced back to your identity or hardware ID.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Cloud database and authentication provider used for optional account sync, Squads, 1v1 Duels, Ranked matchmaking, and anonymous percentile ranking.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes optional Olen PRO subscriptions and in-app purchases. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local app data remains solely on your device and is permanently removed if you clear Olen's app storage or uninstall the app.

How to delete your cloud data or Olen account:

  • In-App: Open Olen → Settings / Account and use the account/cloud data deletion option to erase your synced profile, backups, and match telemetry.
  • By Email Request: Email us at app.olenapp@gmail.com with the subject OLEN Data Deletion Request and include the email address or Squad nickname associated with your account. We will permanently delete all associated cloud records within 7 days and confirm once completed.

7. Children's Privacy

Olen is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it immediately.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & Global)

Depending on your location (including the EU/EEA under GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of any personal data we store, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time by disabling permissions in Android Settings or deleting your account.
